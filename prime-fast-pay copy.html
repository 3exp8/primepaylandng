The product

Built for how value moves now, not how it moved in 2015.

One integration replaces a chain scanner, a payout queue, and a spreadsheet reconciling all of it at month end.

Multi-chain routing

One integration reads deposits across TRON, Solana, and every EVM chain you support, then routes payouts back out on whichever of those the recipient actually uses.

Sub-second settlement

A deposit is observed, confirmed, and credited to a customer's balance before the block finishes propagating to the rest of your infrastructure.

Compliance-first ledger

Every credit and debit posts to a double-entry ledger. A payout can't leave until the checks it depends on have actually run — that ordering is enforced, not just documented.

How it works

Four steps, in the order they actually happen.

This is the real sequence a transfer moves through — nothing here is reordered for a pitch.

01

Deposit observed

A chain scanner watches your customer addresses across every supported network and flags an incoming transfer as soon as it confirms — no polling delay, no missed reorg.

02

Ledger entry posted

The amount is written to a double-entry ledger tied to the customer's account. A balance is never just a number in a row — it's the sum of entries you can always trace back.

03

Compliance check runs

The payout can't move until the checks it requires have cleared. That rule is enforced by the code path itself, so a skipped check isn't a bug that can ship.

04

Payout routed

Funds leave on the chain and rail the recipient actually uses, chosen automatically from whatever's fastest and cheapest at that moment.
